Romaji Reader (Android) - one floating "A" button: tap it, the Japanese on screen becomes romaji
(or romaji + English, or English).

Start the reader BEFORE opening your game (Android shows a one-time permission popup on start).
Open the app -> Allow overlay -> Start reader -> Entire screen. Then open the game and tap A.
Tap A again to clear. Long-press A, the tile, or the notification Stop button to turn it off.

Build with no PC: push to GitHub; the workflow in .github/workflows builds the APK and posts it under Releases.
